# Competitor / Market Notes

These notes capture research already used to shape the product direction. Re-verify exact current feature claims if they are used verbatim in the final presentation.

## Aerchain

Relevant product signals:
- sourcing and evaluation already exist in Aerchain's product surface
- supplier workflows historically include structured quote entry
- newer AI positioning includes sourcing agents, response evaluation/normalization and split-award modelling

References:
- https://aerchain.io/
- https://aerchain.io/products/sourcing-agent
- https://support.aerchain.io/support/solutions/articles/82000905748-how-to-submit-a-quote-supplier-walkthrough
- https://support.aerchain.io/support/solutions/articles/82000909199-how-to-create-and-compare-scenario-quote-comparison

Implication for this assignment:
Do not pitch basic `PDF -> table` extraction as the core innovation. The stronger story is auditable normalization, ugly-edge handling and decision trust.

## Globality

Relevant signal:
AI-guided sourcing, response collection/completeness and analysis. Some response flows still rely on structured pricing templates even if supporting documents can vary.

References:
- https://www.globality.com/platform/
- https://www.globality.com/response-collection/

Implication:
A compelling product thesis is **zero-template supplier response** while preserving reliable comparability.

## Levelpath

Relevant signal:
AI-native sourcing workflows, including analyzing proposals/responses in multiple formats and presenting comparison results.

Reference:
- https://www.levelpath.com/blog/how-cbre-used-ai-native-workflows-to-build-modern-sourcing

Implication:
Arbitrary-format extraction is increasingly table stakes. Trust and exception operations matter.

## Keelvar

Relevant signal:
Advanced sourcing optimization and scenario constraints across cost, supplier risk, sustainability, capacity and other rules.

Reference:
- https://www.keelvar.com/sourcing-optimization-software

Implication:
Natural language should compile to constraints, while a deterministic optimizer calculates the award.

## Fairmarkit

Relevant signal:
Mature RFQ workflow and supplier response collection with structured line-level fields.

Reference:
- https://docs.fairmarkit.com/suppliers/suppliers/request-for-quote-rfq/respond-to-a-request-for-quote-event

Implication:
Aerchain's prompt intentionally pushes beyond structured supplier participation.

## SAP Ariba

Relevant signal:
Traditional enterprise sourcing has strong structured bid analysis and award-scenario capabilities.

Reference:
- https://help.sap.com/docs/strategic-sourcing/event-management/bid-analysis

Implication:
The differentiating UX can be framed as: **accept chaos, create structure, preserve evidence.**

## Lightweight AI quote comparison tools

Examples:
- https://vequora.com/
- https://www.meshworks.com/free-tools
- https://www.procuremind.io/extract

Relevant signals:
Document extraction, line-item comparison and confidence/review workflows are becoming broadly available.

Implication:
The demo should go beyond document ingestion to show a buyer can act on the output with confidence.
